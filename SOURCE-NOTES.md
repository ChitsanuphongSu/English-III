## 1. Source Documents
1. **`Eng 3 – Unit 3 – Multi word verbs.pdf`**
   - Multi-word verbs classification into 4 Types:
     - **Type 1 (Intransitive: No object)**: `wake up`, `go away`, `fall down`, `stay up`, `break up`, `sit down`, `take off`, `calm down`
     - **Type 2 (Transitive: Object can be between or after)**: `wake sb up`, `take sth off`, `calm sb down`, `try sth out`, `figure sth out`, `make sth up`, `throw sth away`, `pick sth up`, `let sb down`
     - **Type 3 (Transitive: Object immediately follows preposition)**: `look into sth`, `focus on sth`, `believe in sth`, `live for sth`, `be into sth`, `look after sb`
     - **Type 4 (Three-word verbs: Two prepositions + object)**: `come up with sth`, `look down on sb`, `look up to sb`, `run out of sth`, `fall out with sb`, `go on about sth`, `get away with sth`

   - **Page 2 & 3 (Unit 3 Grammar)**:
     - Present Perfect vs Present Perfect Continuous:
       - **Present Perfect**: `have/has + V3` (Result of completed action, number of times, e.g., "I have read Harry Potter 5 times", "She has finished her report", clues: *5 times, 3 books, already, yet, ever, never*).
       - **Present Perfect Continuous**: `have/has + been + V-ing` (Activity, process, duration, continuous action, e.g., "I have been reading for the whole day", "They have been working on this project since Monday", clues: *for hours, since morning, all day, recently*).
       - **Stative Verbs**: Stative verbs such as `know`, `love`, and `understand` are generally not used in the continuous form in this comparison (e.g., *"I've known her for years"* instead of *"I've been knowing her"*).

   - **Page 5 & 6 (Unit 4 Grammar)**:
     - Unit 4A: `used to` vs `would`, `anymore`, `no longer`, `be used to`, `get used to`:
       - `used to + base verb`: Past habits/states that no longer happen. Negative/question: `didn't use to`, `Did you use to...`.
       - `would + action verb`: Past repeated actions only (not past states).
       - `be used to + noun / V-ing`: Familiarity / accustomed to.
       - `get used to + noun / V-ing`: Process of becoming accustomed to.
       - `anymore` / `no longer`: No longer happening (*doesn't ... anymore*, *no longer ...*).
     - Unit 4B: "Obligation and permission" (p.49):
       - `be supposed to + base verb` (should / expected)
       - `be forced to + base verb` (compelled)
       - `be allowed to + base verb` (permitted)
       - `let someone + base verb` (allow without "to")
       - `must`, `should`, `ought to`, `need to`, `didn't need to`, `wasn't allowed to`.
