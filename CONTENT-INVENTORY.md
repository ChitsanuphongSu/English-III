# CONTENT INVENTORY — English III Final Exam

## 1. Units & Syllabus Mapping
- **Unit 3**:
  - **Vocabulary 3A**: Ability & Achievement
    - Words: `talent`, `skill`, `potential`, `attitude`, `able`, `successful`, `exceptional`, `skilled`, `cheer` / `cheered`, `victory`, `referee`, `competitor`, `outstanding`, `spectators`, `represent`, `competitive`, `opposing`, `brilliant`, `talented`
    - Collocations: *outstanding at*, *talented for*, *the ability to*, *natural talent for*, *achieve victory over*, *spectators in stadium*, *represent country*, *opposing team*
  - **Grammar 3A**: Multi-word Verbs (4 Types)
    - Type 1 (Intransitive): `wake up`, `go away`, `fall down`, `stay up`, `break up`, `sit down`, `take off`, `calm down`
    - Type 2 (Transitive separable): `wake sb up`, `take sth off`, `calm sb down`, `try sth out`, `figure sth out`, `make sth up`, `throw sth away`, `pick sth up`, `let sb down`
    - Type 3 (Transitive inseparable): `look into sth`, `focus on sth`, `believe in sth`, `live for sth`, `be into sth`, `look after sb`
    - Type 4 (Three-word verbs): `come up with sth`, `look down on sb`, `look up to sb`, `run out of sth`, `fall out with sb`, `go on about sth`, `get away with sth`
  - **Grammar 3B**: Present Perfect vs Present Perfect Continuous
    - Present Perfect: `have/has + V3` (Results, completed actions, quantity, counts like *5 times*, *3 books*, *already*, *yet*)
    - Present Perfect Continuous: `have/has + been + V-ing` (Duration, ongoing activity, *for hours*, *since Monday*, *all day*)
    - Stative Verb Rules: Stative verbs such as `know`, `love`, and `understand` are generally not used in the continuous form in this comparison (e.g. *"I've known her for years"*).

- **Unit 4**:
  - **Vocabulary 4A**: Cause and Result
    - Words & Collocations: `results in`, `led to` / `lead to`, `had an effect on` / `have an effect on`, `affect` (+ direct object), `result` (*as a result of*), `cause` (verb / noun), `influence`
  - **Vocabulary 4B**: Talking about Difficulty
    - Words: `gruelling`, `rigorous`, `arduous`, `tricky`, `tough`, `awkward`, `punishing`, `struggle`, `backbreaking`, `delicate`, `straightforward`
  - **Grammar 4A**: Past Habits & Accustomed Expressions
    - `used to + base verb`: Past state or habit no longer true (*didn't use to*, *Did you use to...*)
    - `would + action verb`: Past repeated action only
    - `be used to + noun / V-ing`: Accustomed to
    - `get used to + noun / V-ing`: Becoming accustomed to
    - `anymore` / `no longer`
  - **Grammar 4B**: Obligation and Permission
    - `be supposed to + base verb`: Expected / meant to
    - `be forced to + base verb`: Compelled
    - `be allowed to + base verb` / `wasn't allowed to`: Permitted
    - `let someone + base verb`: Allow (bare infinitive)
    - Modal verbs: `must`, `should`, `ought to`, `need to`, `didn't need to`
